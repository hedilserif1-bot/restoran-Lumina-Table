OUR STORY

From five continents to one table.

Lumina Table is a meeting place for culinary traditions. Our kitchen draws on techniques and flavours from around the world, guided by what is fresh, thoughtful, and in season.

Every plate is an invitation to discover something new—and to feel right at home while you do.
